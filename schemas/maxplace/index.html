MAXGEN schema · v2.1 · new in 2.0

MaxPlace

One MaxPlace is one real place with its history.

Download MaxPlace (2.1) All versions https://opengenealogyai.org/schemas/maxgen/v2/place.schema.json

SHA-256 6940b499ea549f8314227906ae120f90fdc90e536e5824b5f34233a9c16fff4d

Definition

MAXGEN 2.0 -- one MaxPlace document is one real place with its own history (its names, kinds, parent places and coordinates over time), each fact stored once as a claim with evidence so that many claims can point at it.

The schema's own description, word for word.

Fields

The top-level fields of a MaxPlace document. Parts shared by every schema (the date block, the place block, the evidence link, the claim header and others) are defined once in the shared building-blocks file.

FieldTypeMeaning
place_id requireduuidThe place's permanent id: a random UUID created once by the writer (an import_file, resolve_conflict or build_tree task, R-24), never computed from a gazetteer or other outside id, and kept through renaming, re-typing an
redistribution_license requiredlicenceThe licence under which this place document may be shared; when it is unclear, ask and never guess (RULES 7).
name_assertions requiredarray of name_claimThe names this place has had, at this level only (not the parent places), each a claim with its own evidence; at least one is required.
kind_assertions array of kind_claimWhat kind of place this was over time (a territory that became a state, a village that became a city), each a dated claim with its own evidence.
parent_place_assertions array of parent_place_claimThe places this place lay inside over time, in one or more hierarchies (civil, church, judicial, census ...), each dated as closely as the evidence allows and each a claim with its own evidence; imported parents stay pro
coordinate_assertions array of coordinate_claimWhere this place is on the earth, each with an uncertainty radius and its own evidence; several are allowed for places that moved.
related_place_assertions array of related_place_claimLinks to other places this one was formed from, split from, merged into, succeeded or preceded, each a dated claim with its own evidence; a new county or a split is a new place, linked here.

What changed from 1.13

A slim place authority: one real place with its names, kinds, parent places (dated, with evidence) and coordinates (with their uncertainty when the evidence states one, never invented) over time, related places (formed from, split from, merged into, succeeded by, preceded by), gazetteer ids as outside ids, and undoable merges and distinct_from. Every place claim cites evidence (a gazetteer page is saved as a record first). Made only by import and analysis tasks.
