MAXGEN schema · v2.0 · new in 2.0

MaxGroup

One MaxGroup is one historical group (its own facts only; no member list).

Download MaxGroup (2.0) All versions https://opengenealogyai.org/schemas/maxgen/v2/group.schema.json

SHA-256 8dcdd51571717a6ae38eb9c6378ec0ae140423cd8999b548beee78045ba34657

Definition

MAXGEN 2.0 -- one MaxGroup document is one historical group (an emigrant company, a regiment, a congregation, a household followed across documents) holding only the group's own facts, once, and no member list, because each membership is a claim on the person with group_id.

The schema's own description, word for word.

Fields

The top-level fields of a MaxGroup document. Parts shared by every schema (the date block, the place block, the evidence link, the claim header and others) are defined once in the shared building-blocks file.

FieldTypeMeaning
group_id requireduuidThe group's permanent id: a random UUID created once by the writer (an import_file, resolve_conflict or build_tree task, R-24); merges and splits are made only by analysis (R-28).
is_living_flag requiredbooleanLiving A for the group file itself: true when its period or its names (such as 'the Mossbury household') touch someone who may be alive; when unsure, true; never worked out from members; a floor that only rises; true req
redistribution_license requiredlicenceThe licence under which this group document may be shared; must be tier2-private when is_living_flag is true.
group_kind requiredenumWhat kind of historical group this is (there is no kind for a research cluster of friends, associates and neighbours: those stay as MaxTask results); when no value fits, write other and give the words in group_kind_as_wr
group_kind_as_written stringThe source's or program's own words for the kind of group.
group_kind_uri uriThe address of the kind in a published vocabulary of group types, when there is one.
name_assertions requiredarray of name_claimThe names the group was known by, each a claim with its own evidence; at least one is required.
fact_assertions array of fact_claimThe group's own dated facts (organized, departed, arrived ...), each stored once here with its own evidence and never copied onto members; a member's page shows those inside the membership period at read time.
parent_group_assertions array of parent_group_claimLarger groups this group belonged to during a period (a company inside a regiment), each a claim with its own evidence.
observed_in array of observed_in_itemThe documents that describe the group, including ones that name none of its members.
media_refs array of media_refPhotographs and other files of the group, pointed at by fingerprint on the record that holds each file (each file is described once, on the record that captured it).

What changed from 1.13

One historical group — an emigrant company, a regiment, a congregation, a household that continued across documents — with its kind, its own names and dated facts, the larger groups it belonged to, and the documents that describe it, once. No member list: a membership is a claim on the person. FAN clusters (the friends, associates and neighbours studied around one person) stay as MaxTask results. Made only by import and analysis tasks.
