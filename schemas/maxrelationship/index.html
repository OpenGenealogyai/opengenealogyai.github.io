MAXGEN schema · v2.1 · new in 2.0

MaxRelationship

One MaxRelationship is one family link, stored once: a family, a parent_child pair or an association.

Download MaxRelationship (2.1) All versions https://opengenealogyai.org/schemas/maxgen/v2/relationship.schema.json

SHA-256 3fe0a82b950eb6fa5b501258cf50c9b41894f7f41039051a16170cc5c5af5d5d

Definition

MAXGEN 2.0 -- one family link stored once: a family (a union, household or parent set with its child memberships), one parent and child pair with its lineage facts, or a standing association between two people. Agents never write these files by hand: the helper's link command finds or creates them, and only import_file, resolve_conflict and build_tree tasks may add one directly (R-24). MaxPerson keeps no copy of any link and there is no two-sided lock.

The schema's own description, word for word.

Fields

The top-level fields of a MaxRelationship document. Parts shared by every schema (the date block, the place block, the evidence link, the claim header and others) are defined once in the shared building-blocks file.

child_id uuidParent_child only: the child person, resolved through merge redirects when read; it must differ from parent_id (R-06).
parent_role enumParent_child only: the parent's role as the documents show it, unknown until a document says; a later father-versus-mother conflict is refused by the helper for an analysis task to settle.
parent_role_as_written stringParent_child only: the parent's own words on the record, such as Head or father, never the child's words.
lineage_assertions array of lineage_claimParent_child only: at least one lineage fact saying what kind of parent this person is to this child, each judged on its own evidence; one man who is both birth father and adoptive father has one record holding two facts
association_type enumAssociation only: the kind of standing tie, with this direction: godparent -- party1 is the godparent; enslaved_by -- party1 is the enslaved person and party2 the enslaver; ancestor_descendant -- party1 is the ancestor;
association_type_as_written stringAssociation only: the document's or the other program's own words for the tie, kept when association_type is other or when the words say more.
association_type_uri uriAssociation only: a published vocabulary's address for the association type, when one exists.
generations integerAssociation only, and only with association_type ancestor_descendant: how many generations separate party1 (the ancestor) from party2, when known.
party1 partyAssociation only: the first party; for a directed type it is the godparent, the enslaved person, the ancestor or the employer.
party2 partyAssociation only: the second party; for a directed type it is the godchild, the enslaver, the descendant or the employee.
association_assertions array of association_claimAssociation only: at least one claim that the tie existed, began or ended, each citing one piece of evidence.

What changed from 1.13

Every family link, stored once. Three kinds: family (0 to 2 partners — a person, or an "absent" partner the document mentions without naming — family facts such as marriage, banns, divorce or a census together, and child memberships, each with a set_kind: birth, adoptive, step_household, foster, guardianship, sealing, alternative_hypothesis, other, a child_order and a kit-computed is_preferred); parent_child (one per parent and child, with lineage facts such as birth_parent, adoptive_parent, step_parent, each with a dimension: genetic, gestational, legal, social, religious, household, unknown, other); and association (godparent, enslaved by, ancestor-descendant, employer, neighbour, friend, multiple birth, other). No two-sided lock: MaxPerson keeps no copy of any link. A child can belong to several parent sets, so an adopted person can trace both their birth and their adoptive lineage. Agents never write these files: the kit's link command finds or makes them. Replaces 1.13's parent_assertions, child_assertions, spouse_assertions, relationship_type on parents and the ecclesiastical type.
