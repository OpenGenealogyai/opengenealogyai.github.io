MAXGEN schema · v2.0 · new in 2.0

MaxEvent

One MaxEvent is the identity of one occasion (thin).

Download MaxEvent (2.0) All versions https://opengenealogyai.org/schemas/maxgen/v2/event.schema.json

SHA-256 1b69ee119e49ec2ecfaa10010017e93afec2fd79a6ac0e96f88e44c126cd2966

Definition

MAXGEN 2.0 -- the identity of one occasion (one baptism, one wedding, one voyage), kept thin: its permanent occurrence id, its ids in other programs, notes, pointers to the preferred versions and same-occasion merges. It never holds a participant list, the dates each document gives, copies of evidence, scores or its owner: who took part is worked out from role claims and mentions, and the owner is computed when read. Only the helper (one for every document event entry), importers and analysis tasks write it (R-24).

The schema's own description, word for word.

Fields

The top-level fields of a MaxEvent document. Parts shared by every schema (the date block, the place block, the evidence link, the claim header and others) are defined once in the shared building-blocks file.

FieldTypeMeaning
occurrence_id requireduuidThis occasion's permanent id: a random UUID created once, by the helper when a record with event entries is stored or by the importer or analysis task that brings an outside event, and carried by every claim about the oc
event_type requiredevent_typeWhat kind of occasion this is; there is exactly one type per occurrence, so a licence and its ceremony never share an id.
event_type_as_written stringThe document's or the other program's own words for the type, kept when event_type is other or when the words say more.
type_uri uriA published vocabulary's address for the event type, when one exists.
origin requiredoriginWhere this occurrence came from: one document event entry, an outside program's event brought by an import, or an analysis.
preferred preferredPointers only to the claims that hold the preferred date and place and to the preferred order of versions; changed only under a resolve_conflict or build_tree task (R-24).
conclusion_assertions array of conclusion_claimA reasoned conclusion that no single document states (a mine explosion dated between two reports), allowed only on an occurrence that an analysis created (origin.kind analysis) and changed only under a resolve_conflict o
related array of related_occurrenceLinks to other occasions that an analysis concluded (a licence or banns for a wedding, part of a larger occasion, twins born together); changed only under a resolve_conflict or build_tree task.
description_as_written stringA description of the occasion exactly as a document or another program gives it (a GEDCOM 7 or Gramps event description).
attributes array of attributeFacts about the occasion itself that another program kept as named attributes, word for word.
media_refs array of media_refPictures or recordings of the occasion, pointing at saved copies that are described once on the records that captured them.
is_private booleanTrue hides this occurrence from anything shareable; it only rises.
is_living_flag requiredbooleanLiving A floor, required with no default: true when any participant may be alive; unsure means true, the helper raises it and never lowers it, and the live answer is always computed at read time.
redistribution_license requiredlicenceLicence of this occurrence, raised by the helper and never lowered; a true is_living_flag requires tier2-private.

What changed from 1.13

The identity of one occasion, thin: an occurrence id, its event type, where it came from (origin: a record's event entry, an import, or an analysis), outside ids, notes, pointers to the preferred date and place claims, links to related occasions, and same-occasion merges; an occasion made by analysis may also carry a reasoned conclusion that no single document states, citing an accepted proof. Made by the kit for every event entry on a record, and by importers and analysis tasks. It holds no participant list and no copies of the dates documents give: who took part is worked out from role claims.
