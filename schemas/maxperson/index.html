MAXGEN schema · v2.1

MaxPerson

One MaxPerson is one person, as a researched conclusion (holds no family links).

Download MaxPerson (2.1) All versions https://opengenealogyai.org/schemas/maxgen/v2/person.schema.json

SHA-256 33a42e40d29768874c1def2ba9823bc28455a614bf18efe5a0bfb452f2dee700

Definition

MAXGEN 2.0 -- One MaxPerson is one person as a researched conclusion, every fact about them a claim citing one piece of evidence, holding no family links (parents, children and partners live in MaxRelationship).

The schema's own description, word for word.

Fields

The top-level fields of a MaxPerson document. Parts shared by every schema (the date block, the place block, the evidence link, the claim header and others) are defined once in the shared building-blocks file.

FieldTypeMeaning
person_id requireduuidThis person's permanent random id, created once by the writer and never recomputed or reused.
person_kind enumWhether this is a documented person or a placeholder made only so something (a parent link, a DNA hypothesis, a death) can attach to them; never created under an import task (R-23).
placeholder_label stringPlain words saying who a placeholder stands for, such as "unknown father of Mabel's three daughters"; required when person_kind is placeholder.
is_living requiredbooleanLiving A: whether this person may be alive, with no default (unsure means true); true requires tier2-private, it is a floor that only rises, and privacy is always worked out again when read.
redistribution_license requiredlicenceWhether this person's document may be shared, and under what licence; must be tier2-private while is_living is true.
name_assertions array of name_claimName claims, each citing its evidence; at least one is required unless person_kind is placeholder.
gender_assertions array of gender_claimGender claims, each keeping the document's own words.
birth_assertions array of vital_claimClaims about this person's own birth, always with role principal (another person's part at a birth goes in event_assertions).
death_assertions array of death_claimClaims about this person's own death, always with role principal (another person's part at a death goes in event_assertions).
event_assertions array of event_claimOther events in this person's life and their roles at other people's events; a couple event with role principal, bride, groom, spouse or partner is refused here because it is a family fact written with link (R-22).
attribute_assertions array of attribute_claimLasting facts such as occupation, residence or religion, each citing its evidence.
non_event_assertions array of non_event_claimClaims that something did not happen, such as no marriage by a stated date (GEDCOM 7 NO).
membership_assertions array of membership_claimMembership of a historical group, each citing this person's own line on a roster or similar record (MaxGroup keeps no member list).
photo_assertions array of photo_claimClaims that a face or figure on a photograph record is this person.
external_id_assertions array of external_id_claimClaims that this person is the same as a profile in another system; the only place an outside id for a person is recorded.
attached_sources array of attached_sourceRecords another program attached to this person without naming a fact; research pointers only, never evidence.
same_as_candidates array of same_as_candidatePeople who may be the same as this one but are not merged (GEDCOM 7 ALIA); a merge still needs the merge_person path.

Refused by name: outside_ids. A document that contains this field is not valid.

Added in 2.1

2.1 only adds; every 2.0 MaxPerson stays valid. In the schema file's own words:

  • attribute_claim.attribute_type: new allowed value health_condition
  • attribute_claim.attribute_type (description updated): What kind of lasting fact this is; health_condition (MAXGEN 2.1) is a health condition as written, private by default and never shared.

Everything 2.1 added

What changed from 1.13

Holds no family links. Removed: parent_assertions, child_assertions, spouse_assertions (to MaxRelationship); composite_confidence (a stored score); external_ids (a mirror); dna_evidence (DNA is cited through the evidence link); place_registry (to MaxPlace); source_record_ids (an index); occupation_assertions (now attribute_assertions with attribute_type: occupation); asserted_by, asserted_at, last_updated_at, judge_approved, judge_approved_at (task provenance, kit timestamps and the database's review stage); merge_status (now lifecycle); the flat date keys and country_code/state_province (now the date and place blocks). Changed: every claim gains the claim header and an evidence link; photo claims point at a photograph record. Added: person_kind and placeholder_label, attribute, non-event and group-membership claims, name_parts and name_status, attached_sources, same_as_candidates. is_living and redistribution_license become required, with no default; name_assertions is required for a documented person.
